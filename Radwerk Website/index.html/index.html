Repair Process

From curb rash to curb-ready, on-site.

  1. 01

    On-site inspection

    We check the wheel for how deep the curb rash, chip, or scratch runs before quoting anything — right there next to your car.

  2. 02

    Upfront quote

    You get a flat price for the repair before we start, based on the wheel size, finish, and damage — no surprise charges when we're done.

  3. 03

    Sand & fill

    Curb rash and deep scratches get sanded back to bare metal, and any gouges get filled so the surface is smooth before refinishing.

  4. 04

    Refinish & color-match

    We prime, paint, or clear-coat to match your factory finish, then cure it on-site so it's ready to drive on.

  5. 05

    Final quality check

    We check the finish for a clean color match and a smooth blend into the surrounding surface before we leave, so it looks right up close and from a distance.
